# Hacker News Sample

This page reads the official Hacker News RSS feed and turns it into a compact
mobile-friendly story view.

## What It Does

- fetches the live Hacker News RSS feed on the server
- shows one featured story at the top
- shows a responsive grid of recent stories below
- keeps the page readable on small screens

## What To Observe

- the orange badge makes the source feel distinct
- the story cards are easy to scan on phones
- the feature block gives one story more emphasis

## Useful Notes

- this sample is a good pattern for other headline-only feeds
- you can extend it later with comments or category filters
